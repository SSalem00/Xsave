# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 2026**

XFetch is a browser extension that downloads media from X (Twitter). This policy explains what it does and does not do with your data.

## What XFetch does not do

- XFetch does not collect any personal data.
- XFetch does not track your browsing activity.
- XFetch does not send any information to any external server or third party.
- XFetch does not use analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting.
- XFetch does not store any downloaded media, browsing history, or tweet content.

## What XFetch stores

XFetch saves your own settings — GIF conversion on/off, quality preset, and filename template — using your browser's local extension storage. These stay on your device, are never transmitted anywhere, and are removed when you uninstall the extension.

## What XFetch does

When you click the download button on a tweet, XFetch:

1. Fetches the media URL from X's own public CDN using your browser — the same request your browser would make anyway to display the tweet.
2. For GIFs: decodes and re-encodes the video locally in your browser using an embedded encoder. This process happens entirely on your device.
3. Saves the resulting file to your computer via your browser's built-in download system.

No data leaves your device except the requests made directly to X's servers to retrieve the media you asked to download.
